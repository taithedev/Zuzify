# Zuzify brand

Palette: near-black, soft charcoal, off-white, cool neutral gray. Warmth comes from restraint, thoughtful spacing and motion, not accent color.

Design principles:
- Distinctive oversized typography, readable smaller body text.
- Dark surfaces with thin translucent strokes and subtle glass blur.
- Layers, grid patterns, vignette, drifting snow in the winter theme.
- Strong keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.
- Zuzify = parent brand. Zuzu products = products inside the ecosystem.
